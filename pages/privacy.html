Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Imvula Labs ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Imvula OS platform. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data. We comply with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa.

1. Information Collection and Use

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Service to you. This includes your email address, business name, and the data you input into the platform (such as assets and risks). We do not sell your data to third parties.

2. Security of Data

The security of your data is important to us. All passwords are cryptographically hashed using Bcrypt. Data is transmitted over HTTPS, and our databases are hosted on secure cloud infrastructure (Render). However, remember that no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

3. AI Processing

Imvula OS may use AI models to process data. We do not use your private business data to train public AI models. All AI actions require human approval via the Guardian AI framework.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@imvulalabs.co.za.
