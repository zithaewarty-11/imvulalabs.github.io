Our Assessment Methodology

At Imvula Labs, we follow a rigorous, industry-standard methodology to ensure our security assessments are thorough, accurate, and actionable.

1. Scoping & Reconnaissance

We work with you to define the scope of the assessment (e.g., specific web applications or APIs). We then conduct passive reconnaissance to understand your infrastructure and attack surface without touching your live systems.

2. Threat Modeling

We identify the potential threats relevant to your business model and the data you handle, allowing us to focus our testing on the areas that matter most.

3. Automated & Manual Testing

We use industry-leading automated scanners to identify low-hanging fruit. More importantly, our human testers manually probe for complex business logic flaws, authorization bypasses, and injection vulnerabilities that automated tools miss.

4. Risk Prioritization

We don't just hand you a list of bugs. We classify findings based on the Common Vulnerability Scoring System (CVSS) and map them to business impact, helping you understand what needs to be fixed immediately versus what can wait.

5. Reporting & Remediation

You receive a clear, jargon-free report containing executive summaries for management and technical details for developers. We also provide actionable remediation advice to help your team patch the issues securely.
