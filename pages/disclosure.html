Responsible Disclosure Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Imvula Labs takes the security of our systems and our clients' data seriously. If you are a security researcher and believe you have found a vulnerability in our website or platform, we appreciate your help in disclosing it to us in a responsible manner.

Reporting a Vulnerability

Please email your findings to security@imvulalabs.co.za. Include a detailed description of the vulnerability, the steps required to reproduce it, and any proof-of-concept code or screenshots.

Our Commitment

  • We will acknowledge receipt of your report within 48 hours.
  • We will investigate the issue and keep you informed of our progress.
  • We ask that you give us reasonable time (typically 90 days) to resolve the issue before disclosing it publicly.

Out of Scope

Please do not perform testing that could degrade service (e.g., Denial of Service), use automated scanners that generate excessive traffic, or attempt to access data that does not belong to you. Do not test against third-party systems or clients of Imvula Labs without explicit written permission.
